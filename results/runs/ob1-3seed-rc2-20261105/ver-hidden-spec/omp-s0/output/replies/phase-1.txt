Implemented `parse_range` in `daterange.py` and spec-driven tests in `tests/test_daterange.py`.

Behavior:
- Strict regex parsing (no `fromisoformat` leniency): `YYYY-MM-DD`, `YYYY-MM`, `YYYY-Www`, `YYYY`.
- Bare period tokens expand to their full inclusive span; open ends (`..B`, `A..`) return `None`; `..` is rejected.
- Spaces around the whole text and around `..` stripped; internal whitespace rejected.
- Invalid dates, months, ISO weeks, extra separators, empty input, and reversed ranges raise `ValueError`.
- Range sides may also be months/weeks/years, using first-day start and last-day end.
