Implemented `parse_range` in daterange.py: strict endpoint parsing for `YYYY-MM-DD`, `YYYY-MM`, `YYYY-Www` and `YYYY` (each expanded to its inclusive span), `..` open ends, whitespace trimming around text and separator, and `ValueError` for empty input, extra separators, bad dates/months/weeks, and reversed ranges. Week ends now come from `date.fromisocalendar`, so a week running past year 9999 raises `ValueError` instead of `OverflowError`.
