Implemented `parse_range` in `daterange.py`: a `_term` helper resolves one term to its inclusive first/last day (full ISO date, `YYYY-MM` via calendar month length, `YYYY-Www` via `date.fromisocalendar`, or `YYYY`), and `parse_range` splits on the first `..` and joins the left span's start to the right span's end, allowing open sides and rejecting `..`, unknown terms, and reversed ranges with `ValueError`. tests/test_daterange.py covers every spec clause: dates, months with leap February, ISO weeks including a year-crossing week and 2026-W53, years, open ends, spaces, malformed input and backwards ranges.
